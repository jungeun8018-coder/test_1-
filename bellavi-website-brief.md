# BellaVi Studio Website Brief

## Project

Website for BellaVi Studio.

BellaVi Studio is an art education brand founded by artist and art educator Jungeun Park.

The purpose of the brand is not simply to teach drawing.

Its mission is to help people continue enjoying what they love throughout life.

The emotional keyword of the brand is:

"좋아하는 것을 오래 잃지 않는 삶"

English keyword:

"A life that never loses what you love."

---

# Brand Philosophy

BellaVi Studio believes that people become more beautiful as they continue loving something.

Art is not about drawing perfectly.

Art is a way of slowing down, observing, remembering seasons, and enjoying everyday life.

Visitors should feel calm, warm and inspired.

The website should never feel commercial or loud.

Think of a quiet European café, a small gallery, or an artist's studio.

---

# Target Audience

Primary

- Women aged 50–60
- People who want to begin drawing again
- People preparing for retirement
- People seeking a slower lifestyle
- People interested in travel, nature and art

Secondary

- Adults interested in illustration
- Parents
- Seniors
- Art education organizations

---

# Website Goal

The website should introduce BellaVi Studio and encourage visitors to connect through Instagram, Blog or future classes.

Visitors should leave thinking:

"I want a life like this."

rather than

"I should buy something."
